
=== SOCIAL ENGINEERING AWARENESS SCENARIO ===

Organization: Government
Scenario: Government
Pretext: A caller claims urgent action is required.

[SIMULATION OPENER]
A caller claims that an official document requires immediate attention.

[PSYCHOLOGICAL TRIGGER]
Authority and fear of penalties.

[WARNING SIGN]
The caller demands immediate payment or sensitive personal information.

[SAFE RESPONSE]
Contact the relevant government department through its official website or published contact details.

[TRAINING LESSON]
Pause before acting. Never disclose passwords or
one-time authentication codes. Verify unexpected
requests through an independent official channel.
