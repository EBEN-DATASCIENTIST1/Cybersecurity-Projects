
=== SOCIAL ENGINEERING AWARENESS SCENARIO ===

Organization: Banking
Scenario: Banking
Pretext: A message claims an account needs verification.

[SIMULATION OPENER]
A message claims that a bank account needs urgent verification.

[PSYCHOLOGICAL TRIGGER]
Urgency and fear of financial loss.

[WARNING SIGN]
The message requests banking credentials or directs you to an unfamiliar link.

[SAFE RESPONSE]
Open the official banking app or contact the bank using its verified number.

[TRAINING LESSON]
Pause before acting. Never disclose passwords or
one-time authentication codes. Verify unexpected
requests through an independent official channel.
