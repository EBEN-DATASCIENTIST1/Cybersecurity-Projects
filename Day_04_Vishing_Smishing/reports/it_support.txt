
=== SOCIAL ENGINEERING AWARENESS SCENARIO ===

Organization: IT Support
Scenario: IT Support
Pretext: A caller claims there is an account problem.

[SIMULATION OPENER]
A caller claims to be from IT support and reports unusual account activity.

[PSYCHOLOGICAL TRIGGER]
Authority and fear of losing access to workplace systems.

[WARNING SIGN]
The caller asks for a password or authentication code.

[SAFE RESPONSE]
Contact IT using the official helpdesk channel.

[TRAINING LESSON]
Pause before acting. Never disclose passwords or
one-time authentication codes. Verify unexpected
requests through an independent official channel.
