DAY 12 — LAB JOURNAL

**Project:** Phishing Email Detection with Machine Learning

**Date:** 22 October 2026

**Environment:** Windows, PowerShell, Python, pandas, scikit-learn

**Scope:** Fictional labeled email dataset

**Objective:** Train and evaluate a Naive Bayes classifier for phishing email detection.

**Activities Performed**

1. Created the Day 12 project structure.
2. Installed the required Python libraries.
3. Created a labeled dataset of 20 fictional emails.
4. Implemented a CountVectorizer and MultinomialNB pipeline.
5. Evaluated the initial model.
6. Expanded the dataset to 50 fictional emails.
7. Re-trained and evaluated the classifier.
8. Saved the results to `reports/model_report.txt`.

**Final Results**

- Dataset size: 50
- Training samples: 35
- Testing samples: 15
- Accuracy: 93.33%
- Correct predictions: 14 of 15
- Missed phishing examples: 1
- False positives: 0

**Ethical and Safety Notes**

All email examples are fictional. No real inboxes were accessed, and no messages were sent to real users.

**Conclusion**

The experiment demonstrates a basic machine-learning pipeline for phishing classification. Larger and more representative datasets are necessary before drawing conclusions about real-world effectiveness.
